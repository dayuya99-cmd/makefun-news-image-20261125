An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[news-image-20261125](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125):
one image summing up the world's news of Wednesday 25 November 2026.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png`
(768×1024, 361,810 bytes) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`,
verifying the PNG signature and the copy's sha256.

The six stories: tankers ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz; Houthi strikes
on Saudi Arabia; the Hamas security chief killed in Gaza; Russian strikes
on Odesa and Kyiv; Huawei convicted in a US court; the Macy's
Thanksgiving Day Parade postponed by a storm.
